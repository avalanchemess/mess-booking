Privacy notice

Avalanche Officers' Mess Booking Portal

This portal is used to request and manage guest room bookings at the Avalanche Officers' Mess. This notice explains what the portal collects when you send a booking request and what happens to it.

What we collect

  • Your name and email address from your Google account, when you sign in with Google.
  • What you type into the request form: your name and rank, your phone number, the number of guests and rooms, the names of the guests with their rank or relation, the primary guest's mobile number if you give it, the dates of the stay, and anything you add under other information.

About Sign in with Google

Signing in tells us only your name and email address. The portal never sees your password and cannot read your email, contacts, files or anything else in your Google account.

Why we collect it

  • To consider your request and allot rooms.
  • To contact you about the booking and email you the confirmation.
  • To keep out spam and misuse of the request form.

Who can see it

Only the mess staff who manage bookings. It is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone for advertising or marketing.

Where it is kept

In a private Google Sheet in the mess's own Google account. Emails about your booking are sent through that account's Gmail.

Corrections and removal

Your details are kept with the mess's booking records. To have them corrected or removed, or to ask anything about this notice, email avalanchemess@gmail.com.
